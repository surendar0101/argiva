# Imagery rules

These rules apply to every product, hero and story image on the ARGIVA site.

## Aspect ratios
- Product images and hero images use a 4:5 ratio (portrait).
- Gallery thumbnails are square (1:1).

## Source files
- The long edge must be at least 1600 px.
- The site creates smaller sizes automatically. Upload only the original.

## Backgrounds
- Use plain backgrounds only: a mist-grey surface, or indigo cloth.
- No props that compete with the piece, and no busy patterns.

## Colour
- Show the metal's true colour. Silver must look like silver.
- No warm filters or golden grading on silver.
- Rose-gold or oxidised finishes must look the way the piece does in hand.

## Required shots per product
- Front view on the plain background.
- One hand-held "scale" shot, so buyers can judge the size.
- Close-ups of the hallmark and the clasp are recommended.

## Alt text
- Pattern: `[item], [view]`. For example: "Ghungroo payal, worn on ankle".
- Do not repeat the price or purity in alt text; those are shown as text on the page.

## Sources
- Until ARGIVA's own photos exist, use only the neutral placeholders in `apps/ui-preview/public/placeholders/`, each labelled "Placeholder".
- Never use third-party, stock or supplier photos, and never use photos copied from other websites.
