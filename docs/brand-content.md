### Silver composition rules
- **92.5%:** Describe as "925 sterling silver" only when composition is verified.
- **80% and 60%:** State the verified silver content and explain the remainder is other metals. Never label as 925 sterling silver.
- **Rose-gold finish:** Describe the underlying metal and the finish; never imply solid rose gold.
- Publish hallmark / certification / purity details only when supporting evidence exists.

## 7. Returns & Damaged Products (proposed)

We want you to receive your ARGIVA jewellery in the condition in which it was intended to reach you.

### Damaged Products
If you receive a damaged product, contact ARGIVA within 2 days of delivery.

To submit a claim:
1. Record a clear, continuous video while opening the parcel, showing the outer packaging, package contents and the product.
2. Retain the original packaging and invoice.
3. Contact ARGIVA with your order details and the opening video within the specified period.

All claims are subject to review and approval by ARGIVA. Please contact us as soon as possible so we can assess the issue.

### Resolution for Approved Claims
- If the same product is available, a replacement of the same product may be arranged.
- If the same product is unavailable, you may choose an alternative product of equivalent value to the amount paid for the original item.
- Cash refunds or cash replacements are not offered under this proposed policy.

Any replacement will be coordinated with you after the claim has been reviewed.

### Exchanges
We do not offer exchanges based solely on a change of preference after purchase. If you are unsure about a product before ordering, please contact us for additional photographs, product details or a video call to help you make an informed choice.

### Cancellations
Please contact ARGIVA as soon as possible if you need to request a cancellation. **Cancellation eligibility, particularly after dispatch, must be confirmed before this policy is published.**

## 8. Caring for Your Silver Jewellery

Silver jewellery deserves a little care to keep it looking its best.

- Store your jewellery in a clean, dry place, preferably in a separate pouch or jewellery box.
- Avoid direct contact with perfumes, lotions, household chemicals and cleaning products.
- Remove jewellery before swimming, bathing or strenuous activities, where appropriate for the item.
- Gently wipe your jewellery with a suitable soft cloth after use.
- Take extra care with plated or coloured finishes, including rose-gold finishes. Abrasive cleaners and polishing compounds may damage the finish.

Silver may naturally tarnish over time depending on its composition, finish, storage and exposure to the environment. If your jewellery needs attention, contact ARGIVA to ask about re-polishing options.

### Re-Polishing Service
To request re-polishing, please retain your original ARGIVA invoice or bill. The applicable re-polishing charge will be determined using the prevailing rate at the time of service and communicated before the service is confirmed.

Eligibility, turnaround time and the scope of the service will be confirmed by ARGIVA for the individual item.

### Pricing modes
| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Fixed price | Admin sets the selling price; unchanged until updated. |
| Silver-rate-based | Admin updates the applicable rate and pricing parameters; the system calculates or proposes the price for designated products. |

Rate-based price inputs: silver weight, purity grade (92.5 / 80 / 60), making charges, other metals, finishing, wastage, taxes, margin. Formula may differ per purity grade.

**Critical:** When an order is placed, persist the final agreed price and all pricing inputs with the order. Later rate changes must never alter existing orders.

### Analytics metrics
- **Sales:** revenue, paid orders, average order value, cancellation rate.
- **Products:** bestsellers, slow-moving stock, inventory levels, category performance.
- **Customer behaviour:** product views, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, repeat purchases.
- **Marketing:** Instagram referrals, campaign performance, new vs returning customers, enquiry conversions.
