# Mood comparison for the owner's decision

Business facts this is judged against (from sdd/state.json):
- Family silver business; the story starts with anklets. Brand line: "Modern Silver. Timeless Stories."
- Audiences: women 18–30 (everyday wear), women 30–50 (occasions and gifting), NRI/international buyers.
- Purity varies (92.5 / 80 / 60), so purity must be clear on every card.
- Phone photos at launch, professional photos later. Mobile-first. Budget-conscious.

| Criterion | A Quiet luxury | B Warm Indian craft | C Modern bold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matches "Modern Silver. Timeless Stories." | Strong on both halves | Strong on "timeless"; weaker on "modern" | Strong on "modern"; weak on "timeless" |
| Women 18–30 | Good | Fair: can feel festive or traditional | Strongest |
| Women 30–50 / gifting | Strongest | Strong | Fair: can feel cold |
| NRI / international | Strong: reads as a global premium brand | Strong for the diaspora (heritage pull); fine for others | Good |
| Makes purity clear | Best: hallmark badge | Good: coloured text | Good: rose-gold percentage |
| Tolerates phone photos | Best: calm, muted backgrounds help | Good: warm textured backgrounds hide flaws | Worst: large photos at poster scale expose quality |
| Tells the family story | Medium | Best | Weakest |
| Stands apart from Indian silver competitors | High: few use this palette | Medium: craft and ethnic styling is common | High, but close to Western fashion brands |
| Ages well (2–3 years) | Best | Good | Fair: trend-driven |
| Build and maintenance effort | Low | Medium: SVG ornament, texture | Medium: variable font, scroll rails |
| Supports conversion | Calm and trustworthy, with clear prices | Warm, with a strong call-to-action colour (marigold) | High energy; scroll rails hide products on mobile |

Recommendation: A as the base, borrowing B's ghungroo chain only for the Our Story and Anklets sections.
- Why A: it best suits the mixed audience and launch-time phone photos. Its hallmark badge also solves the varying-purity problem.
- Why borrow from B: the chain adds the heritage story where it matters, without making the whole store feel traditional.
